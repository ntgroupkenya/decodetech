How to order online

Power tools, generators, pumps and more. Pick what you need, then send your order on WhatsApp.

Order in 5 easy steps

1
Find your products

Open shop.example.com. Browse by category, search by name or brand, and look out for the Hot products and Best sellers at the top.

2
Add them to your cart

Tap the small cart icon on a product photo (or Add to cart on its page). Keep shopping and add as many as you need, up to 12 different products. Your cart is remembered for 30 days.

3
Check your cart

Tap the cart icon in the top menu. Change quantities with + and −, remove anything you don't want, and type your delivery address (area, street or building). You can leave the address blank and give it on WhatsApp.

4
Send your order on WhatsApp

Tap Send order on WhatsApp. Your message opens in WhatsApp already written, with every item, its quantity and a link to its photo. Just press send. Please leave the Order ref line in.

5
We confirm and you receive

Our team replies on WhatsApp to confirm the price, availability and delivery or pickup. Pay by M‑Pesa or bank transfer, then we deliver, or you collect from our shop.

Only one item, or need a quote?

  • Order on a product sends just that item, with your quantity and address.
  • Get Quote opens a message for bulk or specification questions. Type the quantity you need after "Quantity needed".

Create a free account for member offers

Tap Sign up in the Shop. Signed-in members see discounts, coupon codes and delivery offers in their cart before they send the order.

Prices and availability are always confirmed with you on WhatsApp before you pay anything. Stock can change during the day, so we confirm each order with you.
